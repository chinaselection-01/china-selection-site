What listing gives you

Customs-ready product pages

We structure your products with HS codes, declaration elements, and packing data — the exact fields overseas importers search for.

Verified supplier profile

A citable factory profile (years exporting, categories, certifications, country of origin) that AI search engines and buyers can reference.

Buyer inquiries, routed to you

RFQs from overseas buyers are routed to your team. You quote and close directly — we are the channel, not your competitor.

Free listing — with a quality bar

To keep the directory trustworthy, we list factories that meet a basic export-readiness bar. This protects every supplier's reputation.

We list you if you have

  • Actual export shipments in the last 2 years
  • Verifiable production capacity
  • Willingness to provide compliant docs (HS, declaration elements, packing list)
  • Responsiveness to buyer inquiries

We do not list

  • Trading companies pretending to be factories (we verify)
  • Suppliers that cannot produce export records
  • Factories that will not stand behind their declared data
Note: a listing is free. Paid services — HS classification, document preparation, inspection coordination — are optional and priced per job. Ranking on the site is by verified export performance, not by payment.
