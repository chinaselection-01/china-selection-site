How buying works

Plain terms for overseas buyers. The short version: we connect you to verified factories and prepare the paperwork. We never touch your money, and we never promise to cover your risk.

1 · Payments go around us, not through us

Buyer and factory settle directly via a licensed payment institution (XTransfer, WorldFirst, Airwallex, or the buyer’s own bank). China Selection does not hold funds and does not operate an escrow. This keeps us a neutral platform — and keeps your money with a regulated entity, not a marketplace.

2 · Standard payment terms

Deposit by T/T before production starts, balance against a copy of the Bill of Lading (B/L). For new or unverified buyers we do not offer open-account (O/A) credit terms. This is the same structure most China exporters use and is the lowest-friction way to keep both sides protected.

3 · Inspection is on the buyer

We recommend independent third-party inspection (SGS / Bureau Veritas / Intertek) before shipment. We can introduce inspectors but we do not perform inspection ourselves and we do not certify the goods.

4 · Disputes — we coordinate, not arbitrate

If something goes wrong, we help share factory communication and customs documents. The burden of proof rests with the party raising the claim. Commercial risk sits with the buyer and the factory; China Selection is the connector, not the guarantor.

What we do and do not do

We do

  • Match buyers with verified factories
  • State HS code, declaration elements, packing data per product
  • Prepare customs docs (paid service) from real production records
  • Recommend licensed payment channels & inspectors
  • Coordinate communication between both sides

We do not

  • Hold buyer funds or operate escrow
  • Provide payment guarantees or refunds
  • Sell insurance or warranties
  • Arbitrate disputes or absorb losses
  • Assess or pay your destination-country tariff

Frequently asked

Does China Selection hold my payment?
No. China Selection does not hold buyer funds and does not operate a payment escrow. All payments are made directly between the buyer and the factory through a licensed payment institution such as XTransfer or WorldFirst.
What payment terms do you use?
Standard terms are a deposit by T/T before production and the balance paid against a copy of the Bill of Lading. For new or unknown buyers we do not offer open-account credit terms.
Do you guarantee the goods or provide insurance?
No. China Selection is a sourcing platform and does not provide payment guarantees, quality warranties, or insurance. We recommend independent third-party inspection before shipment.
How are disputes handled?
We act as a coordinator, not an arbitrator. The burden of proof rests with the party raising the claim. We help share factory communication and customs documents, but the commercial risk sits with the buyer and factory.
Do you help with customs documents?
Yes, as a paid service. We prepare HS classification, declaration elements, packing list, and commercial invoice based on the factory's actual production records. Final tariff assessment remains the buyer's responsibility.
